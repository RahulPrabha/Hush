Hush Support

Help for Hush on macOS 13 and later.

What Hush does

Hush lives in your menu bar and plays ambient noise to help you focus. It has two sounds:

  • Brown: a deep, low rumble, with an adjustable low-pass cutoff from 20 to 500 Hz.
  • Speech Blocker: noise shaped to mask nearby voices.

All sound is generated on your Mac in real time. Hush works fully offline.

How to use it

ActionResult
Right-click the menu bar iconTurn sound on or off
Left-click the menu bar iconOpen the controls: noise type, volume, cutoff, and appearance
Space (controls open)Play or pause
⌘Q (controls open)Quit Hush

Sound fades in and out smoothly. Hush remembers your noise type, volume, and appearance between launches.

Common questions

I don't hear anything

  • Right-click the menu bar icon to make sure sound is on, or open the controls and check the volume slider.
  • Check your Mac's volume and that it isn't muted.
  • Check the output device in System Settings > Sound. If you switched headphones or speakers while Hush was playing, turn Hush off and on again.
  • Brown noise with a very low cutoff is quiet on laptop speakers, which reproduce little bass. Raise the cutoff or use headphones.

The Hush icon is missing from the menu bar

  • Make sure Hush is running: open it from Applications or Launchpad. Hush has no Dock icon, only the menu bar icon.
  • On a crowded menu bar, macOS hides icons behind the notch or when space runs out. Quit a few other menu bar apps, or use a menu bar manager, to reveal it.
  • On newer macOS versions, check that Hush is allowed in System Settings > Menu Bar.

How do I reset my settings?

Quit Hush, open Terminal, and run:

defaults delete com.rahulprabhakar.hush

Then open Hush again. It starts with Brown noise, half volume, and dark appearance.

Does Hush collect any data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.

Get help

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/RahulPrabha/Hush/issues. Include your macOS version and Hush version (select Hush in Applications and choose File > Get Info).
