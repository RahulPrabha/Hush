Hush puts brown noise and a speech blocker in your Mac's menu bar, so focus sound is always one click away. No browser tab, no streaming, no account.

Right-click the menu bar icon to start or stop the sound. Left-click to open the controls.

TWO SOUNDS
• Brown: a deep, rumbling noise for focus and calm. Set its low-pass cutoff anywhere from 20 to 500 Hz to make it darker or brighter.
• Speech Blocker: noise shaped to mask nearby conversation.

STAYS OUT OF YOUR WAY
• Lives in the menu bar, with no Dock icon
• Fades in and out smoothly instead of cutting off
• Volume control with a large, easy-to-read readout
• Light and dark appearance
• Remembers your sound, volume, and appearance

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Hush is fully offline. It makes no network connections and collects no data. There are no analytics and no audio files: every sound is generated in real time on your Mac.
